# Compliance and Design Quality

This project demonstrates basic development controls that support a
standards-oriented semiconductor design workflow.

## Automated Controls

- Git is used for version control and traceability.
- Jenkins automatically runs RTL simulation.
- Jenkins performs design quality and safety-oriented checks.
- Failed checks cause the CI pipeline to fail.

## Standards Awareness

The project considers principles relevant to ISO 26262, such as
controlled changes, traceability, verification, and documented evidence.

JEDEC standards are recognized as relevant semiconductor industry
standards.

This educational project does not claim formal ISO 26262 or JEDEC
compliance or certification. Formal compliance requires additional
requirements, safety analysis, verification, documentation, processes,
and organizational controls beyond the scope of this project.
